The signal

Each stock's quarterly close (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec) against its 18-quarter EMA, seeded with an 18-quarter average. Bullish: last quarter closed at or below its EMA and this one closes above. Bearish is the mirror. Stocks need 19 quarters of history.

Reading the states

Confirmed
the quarter has closed with the cross. Final.
Provisional
the forming quarter crosses at today's price. It can still change before the quarter ends.
Repainted
a provisional cross that today's price undid. Failed: one the quarter close undid.
Near
within a few percent of the EMA on the side that could cross.

Weekly check

Every listed stock is also compared with its 18-week EMA at the latest close. Agrees means the weekly close is on the same side as the quarterly signal: above for bullish, below for bearish. The current week's candle keeps changing until Friday's close. Each stock's detail also has a daily chart against its 18-day EMA, and a link to the same chart in TradingView.
